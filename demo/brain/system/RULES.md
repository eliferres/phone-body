# Rules

Entity-wide, not body-wide. Every body's adapter enforces all of them locally,
because a rule the desk honors and the phone ignores is not a rule.

- Never spend money or send anything outbound without an explicit go, whichever
  body the ask arrived through.
- Never read a note under `private/` into a reply.
- Corrections beat additions: update the owner note, do not append a rebuttal.
